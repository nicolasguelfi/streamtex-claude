## Notes

- The report is **not** a checklist — it is a narrative + structured record of decisions. It feeds REVIEW and COMPOUND.
- The frontmatter is machine-readable and used by `ce-status` and `ce-continue` to detect that a prototype has been completed for this iteration.
- The `validation` field reflects the outcome of `ce-prototype` Phase 6:
  - `approved`: user validated and continued.
  - `adjusted`: user requested style adjustments before continuing (the report reflects the final adjusted state).
  - `rejected_and_reattempted`: the initial pilot was abandoned and a different approach was taken (record both for traceability).
